About Mine Rescue Center

Made by the community, for the community

Mine Rescue Center is a free resource hub for mine rescue teams across the United States. The site features a searchable archive of past competition problems, training resources, and a competition calendar to help teams stay prepared and informed.

Whether you're a responder, trainer, or organizer, Mine Rescue Center is built for you. Have feedback or a feature request? We'd love to hear from you.
